## One-paper independent summary

This meta-analysis summarizes predictors of RAI treatment failure in Graves disease. Its main value is supporting the recurrent finding that thyroid size/volume, uptake, FT4, ATD history and dose-related factors matter, while also showing heterogeneity across studies.
